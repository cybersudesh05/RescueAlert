Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

RescueAlert ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

Location Information: When you trigger an SOS alert, we collect your precise GPS coordinates to include them in the emergency message sent to your contacts. This information is only accessed when the app is in use or when a background SOS alert is triggered.

Contact Information: We allow you to select emergency contacts from your phone's contact list. These contacts are stored locally on your device in a secure database.

Medical Information: Any information you enter into the Medical ID section (blood group, allergies, medications) is stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Information

  • To send emergency SMS alerts to your designated contacts.
  • To display nearby hospitals on a map.
  • To provide first responders with vital health information through the Medical ID.

3. Data Storage and Security

Local Storage: All personal data, including your emergency contacts and medical information, is stored locally on your device. We do not upload this data to external servers.

Third-Party Services: We use OpenStreetMap and the Overpass API to fetch nearby hospital data. These requests are anonymous and do not include your personal identity.

Accessibility Service API

RescueAlert uses the Accessibility Service API to provide a critical safety feature: the ability to trigger an SOS alert by rapidly pressing the volume buttons 5 times. This function is designed to work even when the app is in the background or the screen is locked.

Use and Collection:

  • We use this service only to monitor hardware button press events (volume up/down).
  • We do not collect, store, or share any personal data through the Accessibility Service.
  • We do not read your screen content, track your interactions with other applications, or monitor any other user activity.

4. Permissions

RescueAlert requires several permissions to function effectively:

  • Location: To determine your position during an emergency.
  • SMS: To send alert messages to your contacts.
  • Contacts: To allow you to pick emergency recipients.
  • Phone: To allow auto-dialing of emergency services or contacts.
  • Accessibility Service: To detect rapid volume button presses for SOS triggering.

5. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally, you have full control over it. You can update or delete your emergency contacts and medical information at any time within the app settings.
