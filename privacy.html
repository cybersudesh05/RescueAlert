Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2024

RescueAlert ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share personal and sensitive user data when you use the RescueAlert mobile application.

1. Information We Collect and Why

Precise Location Data: RescueAlert collects precise location data (GPS and network-based) to provide emergency alerting features. This data is collected even when the app is closed or not in use (background location) if a foreground service or SOS trigger is active. We use this to include your coordinates in the SOS message sent to your emergency contacts.

Contacts Data: With your permission, we access your device's contact list to allow you to select and store emergency recipients. This data is stored locally on your device and is only used to identify who should receive your SOS alerts.

Medical Information: Any information you enter into the Medical ID section (blood group, allergies, medications, etc.) is stored locally on your device. This is used to display vital information to first responders in an emergency.

SMS and Phone Data: The app requires permissions to send SMS messages and make phone calls. These are used strictly for the purpose of alerting your emergency contacts and dialing emergency services at your request.

Accessibility Service: RescueAlert uses Android's Accessibility Service API to detect SOS trigger events (such as hardware button presses) even when the app is in the background or the screen is locked. This service is used strictly for the purpose of triggering an emergency response. It does not read, collect, or transmit any on-screen content, personal data, or passwords.

2. Data Sharing

External Sharing: We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal or sensitive data to outside parties. Your location and SOS message are shared only with the emergency contacts you have specifically designated and, where applicable, emergency services.

Anonymous Services: We use OpenStreetMap and the Overpass API to fetch nearby hospital data. These requests are anonymous and do not include your personal identity or persistent identifiers.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Local Storage: All personal data, including contacts and medical information, is stored locally on your device. We do not maintain any of this data on our servers.

Data Deletion: You can delete all your data at any time by:
  • Deleting individual contacts or clearing the Medical ID within the app settings.
  • Using the "Erase All Data" option in the App Settings.
  • Uninstalling the application, which will remove all locally stored data.

4. Permissions

RescueAlert requires several sensitive permissions to function:

  • Location (Background): To determine your position even when the screen is locked or the app is minimized.
  • SMS: To send alert messages to your contacts.
  • Contacts: To allow you to pick emergency recipients.
  • Phone: To allow auto-dialing of emergency services.
  • Accessibility Service: To detect SOS triggers via hardware buttons even when the screen is locked or the app is closed. This service does not read screen content.

5. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally, you have full control over it. You can update or delete your emergency contacts and medical information at any time within the app settings.
